GPT-6.1 Terra in Work and Codex
GPT-6.1 Terra is now available in ChatGPT Work and Codex on Plus,
Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu plans. It’s a fast, efficient model for
professional work that combines strong image understanding with precise
computer use.
Reason about images and screens, then act on what you find across your browser,
desktop, and apps. Terra also provides clear, direct writing that puts results
first and explains supporting details in plain language.
Choose GPT-6.1 Terra from the model list in
Advanced
in the ChatGPT desktop
app or ChatGPT Work on the web. Work also supports Terra on mobile. The model isn’t available in Chat and does not support UAE inference residency. Enterprise access depends on workspace
availability and administrator controls.

Models
for guidance and
browser use
and
computer use
for tool
setup and access requirements.
