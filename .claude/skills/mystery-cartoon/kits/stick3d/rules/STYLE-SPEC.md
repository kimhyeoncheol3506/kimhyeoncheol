# STYLE SPEC (from reference forensics; MEASURED = measured, EST = estimated from frames)
- Format (MEASURED): 720x1280 vertical, 30 fps, ~60 s reference, loudness -14 LUFS, ~35% of time continuous camera motion, scene changes every 4-8 s via 0.2-0.3 s ghost dissolve (EST).
- Palette (MEASURED, reference): teal/green #6b9386, sky blue #849dcc, royal blue #264dba, navy #172353, cream/skin #ceb5a3, off-white #e8e7e9. Saturation 0.43, brightness 0.54. We push saturation +30% (done by the CSS grade).
- Characters (EST): 2D, ink outline ~10-11 px @400px, bean torso (wide), big round head (~27% of canvas width), thin stick limbs with round black hands, round black shoes, big white-ring eyes with small pupils, expressive brows/mouth (grin with teeth, shock O, worry, deadpan). Outfits: suit+tie, leather jacket, blazer, hoodie, shirt; hair: short, swept, bob, grey; glasses, badge, hat.
- World (EST): 3D toon environments: tiled/brick/wood walls with ink grout lines, textured floors, props with readable signage, strong wide-angle perspective (fov ~60-65), colour world per scene (wood-orange / office blue-white / green tile / dusk blue), light shafts and glow.
- Camera (EST): continuous slow dolly/push/orbit, slight roll (+-3 deg), low/high angles, follow shots on travel; hard accent only on key facts.
- Captions (EST): 1-3 words, white extra-bold rounded sans, black outline/shadow, ~65-70% of height, pop per word, no highlight colour.
- Audio (MEASURED): ~200 onsets/min including speech; SFX are light accents under the voice.
- Transitions used: ghost dissolve (default), zoom-in, white flash (for shocks). Add whip/match-cut/wipe variants as needed for variety.
