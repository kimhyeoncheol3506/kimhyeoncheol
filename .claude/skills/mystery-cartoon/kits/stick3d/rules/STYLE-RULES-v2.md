# Approved upgrade rules (apply ON TOP of the finished draft, do not rebuild from scratch)
1. Environment: 15-20 detail props per scene (shelves, plants, posters, tiles, cables, lamps).
2. Lighting: gradient/baked lighting, depth-of-field blur, glow.
3. (4) Textured walls/floors (user's pick: best).
4. FOCUS = stick-figure acting (biggest priority): emotion-driven micro-motion driven by the VO words:
   nervous -> sweat drops that fall, shiver/jitter, trembling hands, darting eyes, gulp; shock -> squash/stretch, jump-back;
   plus idle breathing, anticipation/overshoot on every action, secondary motion (hat/hair/badge lag), gesture library.
5. Camera moves and actions follow the voiceover meaning (push-in on key words, whip on shocks, shake on tension).
6. Color: raise saturation, richer grade (warm/cool split), closer to reference.
7. Process: keep rules; iterate on the existing draft (src/Video.tsx + src/style/*), partial renders only for changed scenes.
Target: ~80% identical to reference.
